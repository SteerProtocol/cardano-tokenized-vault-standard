# Cardano Tokenized Vault Standard Working Group

## Purpose

The working group is an open collaboration led by Steer Protocol to develop
and maintain a Cardano-native tokenized vault standard. Its aim is to make
vaults easier to discover, understand and integrate with across the Cardano
ecosystem.

The work covers common conventions for deposits, vault shares, valuation,
withdrawals and asynchronous settlement, supported by a reference
implementation, conformance tests and integration examples.

## Participation

Participation is open to protocol teams, wallet developers, integrators,
researchers and other interested contributors. Join by opening an issue or
pull request in this repository to introduce your team, share a use case or
propose a contribution. Participation does not require an engineering or
production deployment commitment.

## Coordination and decisions

Steer Protocol coordinates discussions, prepares proposals and maintains the
repository, with input and review from the working group. Contributors can
participate through repository issues, pull requests and working-group calls.

Proposals are reviewed openly, with the aim of reaching consensus. Decisions,
their rationale and unresolved objections are recorded in the repository.
Draft proposals remain proposals until explicitly accepted and documented.

## Open source and maintenance

The standard and its reference artifacts are public goods. Original repository
code and documentation are available under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
Contributors retain ownership of their contributions and submit them under
that licence. Third-party materials retain their existing licences.

The working group supports ongoing review, releases and maintenance, with
Steer Protocol providing initial coordination and repository stewardship.
